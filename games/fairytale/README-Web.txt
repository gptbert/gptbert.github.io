童话拾光 · 十篇 3D 童话游戏 / Tales & Wonder · Ten 3D Fairy Tales

当前合集为 Three.js 网页游戏，无需 Unity。
This collection uses Three.js and does not require Unity.

启动 / Run:
在本目录运行 python3 -m http.server 5176，打开 http://localhost:5176/
Run python3 -m http.server 5176 in this directory, then open http://localhost:5176/.
不要直接双击 HTML。上传整个目录即可静态部署，保留 assets、covers、games 等路径。
Do not use file://. Deploy this entire directory with assets, covers and games paths intact.

中英同时显示。任务、对白、选择和结局支持设备系统朗读，英文优先已有的高品质声音。
Chinese and English appear together. Device voices read tasks, dialogue, choices and endings; English prefers installed high-quality voices.
朗读自动默认关闭，可重播和停止；没有声音也能完整游玩。不需要麦克风，不自动下载语音。
Automatic reading is off by default. Replay or stop any time. Every game remains playable without speech. No microphone or automatic voice downloads.

WASD/方向键移动，E/空格互动，Enter 继续，Esc 暂停；鼠标拖动旋转、滚轮缩放。
WASD/arrows move, E/Space interact, Enter continues, Esc pauses; drag to orbit and scroll to zoom.
手机使用触控方向与互动按钮。每篇进度保存在当前浏览器中。
Use touch directions and interaction buttons on phones. Progress is saved per story in this browser.

这些是明确说明版本的适龄改编，不宣称逐字复原原著。原著差异见每篇入口及 Source-Notes.txt。
These are source-labelled adaptations, not word-for-word reproductions. See each entrance and Source-Notes.txt for changes.

包含十篇可玩故事：灰姑娘、白雪公主、小红帽、丑小鸭、海的女儿、皇帝的新装、睡美人、卖火柴的小女孩、阿拉丁和神灯、美女与野兽。
Includes Cinderella, Snow White, Little Red Riding Hood, The Ugly Duckling, The Little Mermaid, The Emperor’s New Clothes, Sleeping Beauty, The Little Match Girl, Aladdin, and Beauty and the Beast.

Three.js 的 MIT 许可见 Three-LICENSE.txt。角色、场景与对白为项目原创建模/适龄改写；没有使用影视公司角色资产。
See Three-LICENSE.txt for the Three.js MIT license. Character/scene geometry and adapted dialogue are project-authored; no film-studio character assets are used.

手机测试为桌面触控模拟，尚未声称真机或儿童学习效果验证。
Phone testing uses desktop touch emulation; physical-device and child-learning validation are not claimed.
