Privacy Policy

This app respects and protects the privacy of all users.

1. Information Collection

This app does not actively collect users' personal identification information. When users use API keys, model addresses, book sources, conversations, or related settings, that data is used only to provide the app's functions.

2. Use of Information

Settings entered by users are used only for local functions or third-party API services configured by the user. This app does not sell or rent user data or use it for advertising tracking.

3. Third-Party Services

If users configure third-party APIs, model services, or book sources, related requests may be sent to those services. Their handling of data is governed by their own privacy policies.

4. Data Storage

Configuration data in the app may be stored locally on the user's device. Users can remove it by deleting the app or clearing its settings.

5. Children's Privacy

This app does not knowingly collect personal information from minors.

6. Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy as the app's features change. The updated policy will be posted on this page.
