This blog started as a small site. The original pages provided a [privacy policy](/privacy.html) and [support information](/support.html). Now posts have a place of their own, too.

## How it is published

Posts are Markdown files in the [GitHub repository](https://github.com/gptbert/gptbert.github.io). Jekyll builds the site, and updates to `main` publish to `gptbert.com`. GitHub Pages continues to serve the original `gptbert.github.io` address.

Cloudflare Pages gives pull requests a preview URL. That makes it possible to check layout and links before publishing.
